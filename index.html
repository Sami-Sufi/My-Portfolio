Final Year Project CCUS · CO₂-EOR 2023 — 2024

Technoeconomic Evaluation of CO₂ Sequestration in a Sabah Oil Field

BEng (Hons) Petroleum Engineering — Asia Pacific University • Sabah, Malaysia

Investigated the technical and economic feasibility of pairing CO₂-Enhanced Oil Recovery with carbon sequestration in a Sabah oil field — building dual reservoir simulation models (immiscible & miscible) in CMG to benchmark recovery performance against net CO₂ stored, and defining the pathway to the most profitable EOR–CCUS implementation.

Project Timeline — Gantt Chart Dec → Aug

Objectives

  1. 01 Benchmark the difference in recovery factor between immiscible and miscible CO₂ flooding schemes.
  2. 02 Evaluate the net CO₂ sequestered within the reservoir over the injection life.
  3. 03 Propose the most profitable EOR–CO₂ sequestration implementation method.

Scope of Work

  • Conducted a structured literature review of 8+ SPE & journal publications across CO₂-EOR performance, CCS economics, and geomechanical implications to define the research problem and gap.
  • Constructed the field's structural reservoir model (grid-top) and incorporated rock properties, PVT behaviour (lab data + WINPROP EOS characterization) and SCAL curves.
  • Built parallel simulation workflows — black-oil immiscible (CMG IMEX) vs compositional miscible (CMG GEM) — then initialized both models for the comparative study.
  • Designed the techno-economic evaluation chain: primary & secondary economic analyses feeding into net CO₂-sequestrated and NCF calculations.
  • Applied professional engineering practice: HSE risk assessment (induced seismicity, environmental contamination), regulatory compliance for CO₂ capture/transport/storage, and community engagement — with delivery managed via Gantt-chart scheduling at zero software cost (APU CMG academic license).

Phase 1 delivered: structural model constructed, property-populated and initialized. Phase 2 advances to well placement, technical performance and full economic analysis.
